## 6. Who May Access Your Information?

Access to healthcare information may vary according to the user's role, authorization, and relationship with the patient.

### Patient
Patients may access their own relevant healthcare information, referrals, prescriptions, appointments, and follow-up information.

### Health Worker
Authorized health workers may access information required to register patients, conduct assessments, support referrals, coordinate with facilities, track patient progress, and support follow-up.

### Doctor
Authorized doctors may access relevant information necessary for consultation and treatment, which may include patient information, assessment information, relevant medical history, referral details, diagnostic reports, prescriptions, and relevant follow-up information.

### Healthcare Facility
A receiving facility may access relevant information necessary to process and manage an incoming referral and provide care.

### Caregiver
A caregiver may access patient information only when the patient has authorized that caregiver. Caregiver access is intended to be **scoped and revocable** and does not automatically provide unrestricted access to the patient's complete medical record.

### Administrator
Authorized administrators may access operational information required to manage the platform. Access to patient-identifiable clinical information should be restricted to situations where it is necessary and authorized.
